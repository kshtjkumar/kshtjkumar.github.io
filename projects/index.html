Cognitive Neuroscience & Tools

Cognitive Paradigms & Digital Behavioral Phenotyping

Traditional psychological and cognitive batteries often rely on static laboratory assessments that suffer from limited ecological validity. To bridge the gap between computational theory and behavioral experimentation, I develop gamified cognitive paradigms to capture continuous, high-dimensional behavioral phenotypes across visuospatial memory, phonological working memory, executive function, and motor-cognitive stability.

These interactive environments generate rich phenotypic data streams for computational modeling of latent cognitive states and digital biomarker discovery in neuropsychiatric contexts.

Computational Psychiatry · Harvard / McLean

Computational Psychopathology & Reinforcement Learning in Depression

Research with the Computational Psychopathology Group, Center for Depression, Anxiety and Stress Research (CDASR), McLean Hospital – Harvard Medical School (PI: Dr. Poornima Kumar). Developing a scalable battery of dynamic cognitive paradigms to evaluate reinforcement learning (RL) and value-based decision-making behavior across diverse community cohorts.

Conducting high-density EEG and behavioral analyses to isolate reward prediction error signaling and reward-learning deficits in individuals with Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) versus healthy controls. Implementing Bayesian computational cognitive models and machine learning pipelines to delineate latent computational phenotypes of anhedonia.

Neural Interfaces & Biomaterials

Translational Platform for Polyimide Neural Interfaces

Commercial polyamic acid formulations for thin-film flexible neural interfaces lack clinical biocompatibility approval and surgical customizability. We engineered an open-source, end-to-end translational pipeline for synthesizing medical-grade BPDA–pPDA polyimide microelectrode arrays directly from custom polyamic acid precursors.

Evaluated in vivo in epileptic mouse models, these microfabricated probes demonstrate chronic mechanical compliance, low impedance, and high-fidelity local field potential (LFP) recordings across longitudinal seizure monitoring.

Computational Decision Making

Patch Foraging & Belief Updating in Dynamic Landscapes

Investigating how humans balance resource exploitation versus exploration in non-stationary, depleting environments under distinct cognitive constraints. Combining behavioral paradigms with reinforcement learning and Bayesian models, this research isolates information-dependent strategy shifts and working memory capacity limits.

Extended to clinical dimensions to determine how schizotypal traits and psychiatric dimensions modulate belief updating, volatility priors, and adaptive foraging trajectories under uncertainty.

Selected at RLDM (Brown Univ.) & CNLM (UC Irvine) · Submitted
Digital Health & Wearables

Wrist-Wearable Epileptic Seizure Prediction Algorithm

Developing non-invasive forecasting algorithms for wrist-wearable physiological devices to anticipate epileptic seizure onset. The algorithm monitors systemic state transitions using excitation–inhibition (E/I) balance metrics integrated with peripheral autonomic biosignals, including ECG and EMG.

Correlating dynamic pre-ictal and post-ictal electrophysiological signatures with circadian rhythms and sleep architecture establishes robust early-warning windows for ambulatory seizure management.

Preclinical Development · IIT Kanpur
Surgical Devices & DBS

Commutable Coaxial Micro-Macro Electrodes for Human MER

Deep brain stimulation (DBS) neurosurgery typically requires exchanging microelectrodes (for microelectrode recording / MER target localization) with separate macro-stimulation leads. We engineered a commutable coaxial electrode architecture combining cellular single-unit recording and macro-stimulation on a unified surgical shank.

Preclinically validated in rodent stereotaxic models to demonstrate cellular resolution electrophysiology, minimal parenchymal tissue displacement, and rapid commutability for neurosurgical translation.

Preclinical Rodent Evaluation · Pre-submission
Open Hardware & Instrumentation

Automated Operant Conditioning & Drug Delivery Chamber

Commercial operant conditioning systems impose high costs and closed software ecosystems that hinder scalable rodent behavioral experiments. Designed and fabricated an open-hardware, low-cost modular operant chamber for mice featuring automated pellet dispensers, optical lickometers, and precision pharmacological micro-infusion.

Fully integrated with Bonsai and Python microcontrollers for high-temporal-precision reinforcement learning, reward delivery, and state-dependent behavioral tracking.

Electrophysiology & Epilepsy

Aperiodic EEG Dynamics & E/I Balance in Chronic Epilepsy

Evaluated whether 1/f scale-free aperiodic EEG spectral slopes index cortical excitation-inhibition (E/I) balance across progressive network reorganization in chronic epilepsy. Using a genetic mouse model of Lafora disease (Epm2a−/−), aperiodic exponents were tracked across resting wakefulness, anesthesia, and PTZ-induced seizures.

Demonstrated that epileptic networks exhibit systematically steeper aperiodic spectral slopes, validating aperiodic activity as an electrophysiological biomarker of network reorganization and cortical hyperexcitability.

Machine Learning & Psychiatry

Multimodal Machine Learning Biomarkers Across Psychosis

Collaborative computational research with the Computational Psychopathology Group (Harvard Medical School / McLean Hospital) evaluating machine learning pipelines for early biomarker discovery across schizophrenia, schizoaffective, and high-risk clinical populations.

Leveraged high-dimensional datasets spanning cognitive battery performance, structural/functional MRI neuroimaging, and EEG/ERP electrophysiology to improve diagnostic precision and treatment stratification.
