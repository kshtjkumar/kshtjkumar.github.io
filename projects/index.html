Cognitive Neuroscience & Tools

Cognitive Paradigms & Digital Behavioral Phenotyping

Developing gamified cognitive tasks to capture continuous, high-dimensional behavioral phenotypes across visuospatial memory, phonological working memory, and executive function. These paradigms generate rich data streams for computational modeling of latent cognitive states and digital biomarker discovery.

Computational Psychiatry · Harvard / McLean

Computational Psychopathology & Reinforcement Learning in Depression

Developing scalable dynamic paradigms and high-density EEG to evaluate reinforcement learning, reward prediction error signaling, and anhedonia phenotypes across diverse cohorts with the Computational Psychopathology Group (CDASR, McLean Hospital / Harvard Medical School).

CDASR Profile (Harvard Medical School / McLean) ↗
Neural Interfaces & Biomaterials

Translational Platform for Polyimide Neural Interfaces

Engineered an open-source translational synthesis pipeline for medical-grade BPDA–pPDA polyimide microelectrode arrays directly from custom polyamic acid precursors. Validated in vivo in epileptic mice for chronic mechanical compliance and high-fidelity LFP recording.

View in IEEE Access (DOI: 10.1109/ACCESS.2026.3674701) ↗
Computational Decision Making

Patch Foraging & Belief Updating in Dynamic Landscapes

Investigating human resource exploitation vs. exploration in non-stationary environments using reinforcement learning and Bayesian models. Isolates information-dependent strategy shifts and working memory capacity limits under uncertainty and across schizotypal traits.

Selected at RLDM (Brown Univ.) & CNLM (UC Irvine) · Submitted
Digital Health & Wearables

Wrist-Wearable Epileptic Seizure Prediction Algorithm

Non-invasive forecasting algorithms integrating cortical excitation–inhibition (E/I) balance metrics with peripheral autonomic biosignals (ECG/EMG) to establish anticipatory early-warning windows for ambulatory seizure prediction and circadian monitoring.

Preclinical Development · IIT Kanpur
Surgical Devices & DBS

Commutable Coaxial Micro-Macro Electrodes for Human MER

Engineered a commutable coaxial electrode architecture integrating cellular single-unit recording and macro-stimulation on a unified surgical shank for deep brain stimulation (DBS), validated in preclinical rodent stereotaxic models.

Preclinical Rodent Evaluation · Pre-submission
Open Hardware & Instrumentation

Automated Operant Conditioning & Drug Delivery Chamber

Low-cost, open-hardware modular operant chamber for mice with automated pellet dispensers, optical lickometers, and precision pharmacological micro-infusion integrated with Bonsai and Python microcontrollers for reinforcement learning tasks.

Electrophysiology & Epilepsy

Aperiodic EEG Dynamics & E/I Balance in Chronic Epilepsy

Evaluating 1/f scale-free aperiodic EEG spectral slopes as an electrophysiological biomarker of cortical excitation-inhibition (E/I) balance and progressive network reorganization across resting wakefulness and seizures in genetic mouse models.

View Preprint on bioRxiv ↗
Machine Learning & Psychiatry

Multimodal Machine Learning Biomarkers Across Psychosis

Collaborative computational research evaluating machine learning pipelines leveraging high-dimensional cognitive battery scores, MRI neuroimaging, and EEG/ERP electrophysiology for early biomarker discovery across schizophrenia and clinical high-risk populations.

Published in Biomarkers in Neuropsychiatry (Elsevier) ↗
